01

Flow

SUTD · Architecture Core Studio Exercise 01 · Paper tectonics One sheet, 250 × 250 mm

Flow begins with a single 250 mm sheet of paper and three permitted operations: cuts, folds and creases. The surface is divided into a field of strips whose widths drift wider toward one edge, then each strip is pushed to the limit of how many folds and splits one plane can carry. What results reads as topography: a folded terrain that ripples from tight pleats into open ground, generated entirely from one flat sheet. Scaled up, the same logic reads as a canopy of shifting light and shadow.

02

Eclipse

SUTD · Architecture Core Studio Exercise 02 · Light & section Positive & negative massing models

Eclipse is an inhabitable void carved beneath a sweeping landform. Curved trapezoidal apertures open through the terrain above, each one tuned to draw daylight into the wide gathering space below. As the sun moves across the sky, the skylights cast slow, shifting crescents through the interior, turning the section itself into a kind of sundial. Developed through paired positive and negative models, the project treats emptiness, rather than mass, as its primary material.

03

Gardenáf

SUTD · Architecture Core Studio Exercise 03 · Geometry & accessibility Circle · Square · Equilateral triangle Client · Retired gardener, 65

Gardenáf answers a single client: a 65-year-old woman who has chosen a slower life around her garden and needs a home she can move through without stairs or strain. The plan is built from three primitives, a circle, a square and an equilateral triangle, then lifted into massing. The triangle anchors the composition while the circle and square shift in size and position until the figure resolves into four corner rooms around a central circle. That circle becomes the kitchen and the heart of the house, opening straight onto the garden, with every room reached from it on one level. The square is subtracted to leave a sheltered corner for parking, close at hand for limited mobility. Rigorous geometry, turned entirely toward care.

The client

I'm 65, and I've chosen to live alone in a quiet cottage, away from the rush of the city. My hip isn't as strong as it was, so I move carefully and avoid stairs and long walks, but I'm determined to stay independent. Gardening gives my days structure: I tend my herbs and vegetables each morning and cook with what I've grown. On weekends my children and grandchildren visit, and I cook for them from my own harvest. The garden is my pride, my routine, and the heart of my home.

2.1

A Crappy Situation

SUTD · Spatial Design World Exercise 1 · Floor plan Modelled in Rhino

Exercise one asks for a floor plan of a house and the life lived inside it. Mine includes a dog. The plan documents what sharing a home with a pet actually involves, down to the daily negotiations over territory, while the animation makes the point directly: my dog, my garden, and a mess to clean up on a regular basis. A crappy situation, literally.

2.2

Two's a Crowd

SUTD · Spatial Design World Exercise 2 · Interior isometric Modelled in Rhino

Exercise two re-runs the same scenario in a tiny apartment room rather than a landed house. The interior isometric resolves the logistics in full: belongings hung on the wall and out of paw's reach, a step that lets the dog reach the bed, and a dedicated bed and toys so she has a space of her own. The animation works through how the room is packed and rearranged around her. Two residents, one room, every element earning its place.

2.3

Block Axonometric Perspective

SUTD · Spatial Design World Exercise 3 Modelled in Rhino

The final exercise zooms out from the room to the entire block. The brief: model the surrounding neighbourhood as a set of legible solids, cutting doors, windows, kerbs and street lines just deep enough to anchor the room in its urban fabric, then use colour to pick out a single slice so it reads against the muted city around it. Here that slice is my own apartment, a single shaded window marking exactly where the earlier room sits. Private interior and public city, finally drawn at one scale.
